This site is a technical blog run by **Jiwon Min**. It covers web development, infrastructure, and AI coding tools. The goal is not only step-by-step tutorials, but also **failure points, decision criteria, and limits found in real work**.

## Topics

- Local and cloud development setups (Docker, WSL2, Ubuntu, AWS, and more)
- Operations, observability, and deployment notes
- Cost, context, and workflow limits when adopting AI coding tools on a team
- Weekly AI digests focused on source links and practical impact

Posts aim to explain **why a setup was chosen**, **what broke**, and **how to avoid repeating the same mistake**.

## How posts are produced

1. **Topic selection**: Prefer issues I hit myself, configurations I keep re-searching, and tool-adoption decisions.
2. **Drafting**: Some posts use AI tools for drafting or structure. When that happens, the post discloses it at the top.
3. **Review and verification**: Commands, settings, links, and reproduction steps are checked by the operator before publish. Unverified numbers or steps are omitted or marked as such.
4. **Languages**: Korean is the primary source. English, Japanese, and Chinese versions may be published after review for meaning.

AI-generated images are labeled. Advertising and analytics follow the [Privacy Policy](/en/privacy-policy/).

## For readers

- Before copying commands, adapt them to **your versions, permissions, and network**.
- Corrections and improvements are welcome by email or GitHub issues.
- Content is for learning and practical reference, not official product documentation.

## Site standards

- Prefer original experience and verifiable procedures.
- Placeholder or demo-only bodies and low-value duplicates are not published, or are taken offline.
- Ads are limited so they do not outweigh article content.
